## 🔬 Research Experience

### 🧠 NeuroVentura — Multimodal Alzheimer's Disease Risk & Progression Prediction
#### **Supervisor**: Md. Saiful Bari Siddiqui (Senior Lecturer) **Co-supervisor**: Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda (Professor)
**Undergraduate Thesis Research | BRAC University | 2026**  


Worked on a multimodal machine learning framework for predicting Alzheimer's disease risk and progression by integrating medical imaging and clinical data.

- Developed a late-fusion architecture combining a frozen **3D DenseNet-121 MRI model (MONAI)**, **XGBoost** for clinical and demographic features (MMSE, age, and gender), and a **Residual MLP**.
- Worked with 3D medical image analysis and deep learning using PyTorch and MONAI.
- Applied multimodal feature integration and machine learning techniques for healthcare prediction.
- Explored model evaluation and interpretability for clinical prediction tasks.

**Research Focus:** Medical Image Analysis, Alzheimer's Disease Prediction, Multimodal Machine Learning, Deep Learning, 3D CNNs, XGBoost, PyTorch, MONAI.
